Rules of this experiment

  • Each pixel holds temperature and water. Temperature stays fixed, with colder air toward the top.
  • Each tick, water above the local threshold triggers a downward transfer. Warmer pixels have higher thresholds and can stop a streak.
  • A triggered pixel sends the Fall transfer fraction of its total water to the pixel below, keeping the rest.
  • All pixels update together: water travels at most one pixel per tick. Water crossing the bottom leaves the grid and counts as drained.
  • Water starts in noisy upper patches. No new water is added, so the run eventually settles.

Local threshold = Threshold × (0.55 + 0.9 × temperature), where temperature ranges from 0 to 1. This is a simplified rule experiment: no wind, evaporation, or separate droplets yet.
