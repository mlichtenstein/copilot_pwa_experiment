Rules and architecture

This is a stylized discharge-growth experiment, not an electrical solver. Each growth step scores seven candidate directions: score = downwardBias × cos(angle) + roughness × (seededNoise + 0.7 × random). The highest score extends the channel; branchChance can emit a short side branch. Reaching the terrain boundary marks the ancestor path for a brighter flash. Flash energy decays exponentially over flashDuration, then waits interval seconds before growing again. This is a timer, not a simulated buildup of electrical charge. Leader duration depends on channel length and leaderSpeed; the bright flash begins on contact with terrain, not at a predetermined timestamp.

The scene module owns JSON-serializable state, seeded randomness, dynamics and a read-only renderer. A reusable host owns the fixed-step clock, sliders generated from parameter metadata, fresh setups/reset, rating bar, sharing and canvas. No Lightning-specific branches live in the host. See the scene contract.

Geometry, field scoring and illumination are artistic approximations. No electrostatic solve, leader polarity, upward streamer, charge conservation or thunder propagation is modeled yet. Render resolution is explicit at 160×100; the display enlarges pixels without changing dynamics.
