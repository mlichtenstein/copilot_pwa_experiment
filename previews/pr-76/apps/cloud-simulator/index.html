Welcome to my sunset simulator experiments. I'm trying to learn what makes sunsets beautiful, and to build a system that generates sunsets with interesting weather. The goal is something that feels like watching real, beautiful weather, but keeps surprising you.

You can help: as you play around, use the emoji bar (❤️ 🎨 🤮 ⬜) at the top of each page to tell me when a scene is lovely, gross or boring. That feedback shows me which settings are worth exploring further. Have fun!

ⓘ Hold any button or control to see what it does.

Experiments

    How the pages work

    The experiments. Each page is one small idea about clouds and light, simulated live in pixels. The picture is the point: it takes all the room it can, with the settings beside it (below it on a phone).

    The common panel. Every page has the same controls first. Play and Step run its one clock. Randomize gives every setting a random value. Drift (on by default) lets the sliders wander while playing, as fast as driftSpeed says. New seed reshuffles the randomness. timeOfDay moves the shared sun; after sunset the page goes dark. The experiment's own settings come next, labelled with their code names.

    Hold for help. Descriptions stay hidden so the picture can be big. Press and hold a control (or hover it, or tab to it) to see what it does.

    Ratings and links. Above each picture, tap ❤️ 🎨 🤮 or ⬜ when a look makes you feel something: it saves the settings, your rating and a small picture for everyone to see under Recent. 🔗 copies a link that reopens exactly what you see. ❤️ Random opens one of everyone's ❤️ looks.

    One sky. The time of day, the sun's path and Drift come with you from page to page (a 🔗 link keeps its own).
