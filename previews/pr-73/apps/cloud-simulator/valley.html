What is moving?

Sculpted bank and Layered wisps come from real CC0 photographs by Free Nature Stock and George Becker. On load, they become grayscale shading plus an alpha mask: source RGB is discarded. A soft blue-sky key removes gaps in Sculpted bank; a soft red-channel brightness key removes luminous sky around the dark Layered wisps. These source-specific mattes are approximate, not semantic cloud recognition; similar-colored sky/cloud may be misclassified. The renderer supplies all color. Original grayscale maps remain available.

Windward and leeward clearance affect opposing uphill/downhill slopes according to wind direction. Clearance follows the visible hillside silhouette, fades on gentle slopes, and vanishes on flats, at peaks and in calm wind. Every cloud layer shares that boundary, so hidden hills cannot cut holes in open sky. Set one side to zero to let clouds creep to that slope while clearing the other. This is an artistic terrain interaction, not an orographic airflow solver.

Two independent 3D gradient Perlin fields evolve through time and slide in opposite directions. Their product controls reveal: A = Perlin(x·noiseScale − phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale, phase·0.17); B = Perlin(x·noiseScale·1.7 + phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale·1.7, −phase·0.11); phase = time·formationSpeed. Each field also has a fixed seed and layer offset. For speed, each layer samples a 33×21 product grid and interpolates it across the image. reveal = smoothstep(0.5·(1−coverage) − maskSoftness, 0.5·(1−coverage) + maskSoftness, A·B). Coverage 0/1 explicitly clears/removes the cutout. Legacy ribbon/oval masks remain selectable.

alpha = bitmapOpacity × opacity × heightBand × slopeClearanceMask × reveal. Bitmap opacity uses the extracted cloud alpha; source grayscale supplies shading only. Brushes drift at three rates with seeded position jitter, independent scale, flips and small rotations. Photos appear once on the back sky plane, using an aspect-preserving crop. Full sky removes the height band; other placements choose separate centers. Photo wind continuously advects a wrapping sky strip. At load, an 18% horizontal overlap softly blends the original left/right edges in opacity and opacity-weighted grayscale, avoiding a hard seam or empty fade stripe. The strip stays on one back plane and is never mirrored. Its overall pattern eventually repeats. New brush layout changes only brush placement and variation, not photos or time. This is an artistic collage, not a fluid simulation. Two selectors choose Cloud A/B; textureBlend crossfades their opacity and opacity-weighted grayscale shading before applying reveal and lighting. Transparent source pixels contribute no shading. Reset restores Sculpted bank / Billowy bank, blend 0, Low rolling hills, Full sky, Perlin reveal, brush layout 1 and time 0. Background tabs do not catch up.
