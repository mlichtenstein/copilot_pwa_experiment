How this sketch fits together

Three distance layers hold cloud water, humidity, rain, and independent charge/channel state. The same cloud water supplies atmospheric cloud bodies, layered visibility silhouettes and rain formation. Fixed grayscale bitmaps mix with evolving noise; wind moves them in a common perspective camera. Horizon’s atmospheric paths color different altitudes. Light veils are drawn first, then far-to-near clouds, rain, lightning and terrain.

Weather advances in fixed 0.05-second ticks. Drawing aims for four new images per second; grids and lighting have different resolutions. The timing readout gives rolling median/p95 milliseconds for dynamics, lighting and drawing. These are local CPU measurements, not phone benchmarks.

This is an experiment: humidity/reveal and rain are artistic normalized fields, with no water conservation or full convection solve. Lightning reuses its own charge system; its coordinates are warped into this camera and its charge is not yet coupled to the visible cloud water. Terrain and trees are stylized. Saved links restore settings and internal seed and restart weather at t=0, not the current live frame. See composition and limits.
