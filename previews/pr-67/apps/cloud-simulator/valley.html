What is moving?

Rose & slate and Ember bands are crops of real CC0 sunset photographs by Free Nature Stock and George Becker. Their RGB color is retained. These are photographic sky patches, including some clear sky, not physically isolated cloud density. The three original grayscale maps remain available.

Two independent 3D gradient Perlin fields evolve through time and slide in opposite directions. Their product controls reveal: A = Perlin(x·noiseScale − phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale, phase·0.17); B = Perlin(x·noiseScale·1.7 + phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale·1.7, −phase·0.11); phase = time·formationSpeed. Each field also has a fixed seed and layer offset. For speed, each layer samples a 33×21 product grid and interpolates it across the image. reveal = smoothstep(0.5·(1−coverage) − maskSoftness, 0.5·(1−coverage) + maskSoftness, A·B). Coverage 0/1 explicitly clears/removes the cutout. Legacy ribbon/oval masks remain selectable.

alpha = bitmapOpacity × opacity × heightBand × mountainClearanceMask × reveal. Photo opacity uses layer weights 0.9/0.35/0.25; grayscale maps use intensity. Three layers drift at different wind rates. Full sky removes the height band; other placements choose separate centers. Photographs mirror at sampling borders instead of hard wrapping. This is an artistic collage, not a fluid simulation. Reset restores Rose & slate, Low rolling hills, Full sky, Perlin reveal and time 0. Background tabs do not catch up.
