The rules and update formulas

Each pixel holds water (dimensionless water content) and temperature (normalized from 0, cold, to 1, warm). The slider names below match the model parameters. These are experimental quantities, not calibrated humidity or degrees Celsius.

For each pixel, on each tick:

localThreshold = baseThreshold × (0.55 + 0.9 × temperature)

falling = water > localThreshold ? water × transfer : 0

waterNext = water − falling + fallingFromPixelAbove

All right-hand values come from the old grid. Water moves at most one pixel per tick. The top receives no incoming water; outflow at the bottom is added to drained. Temperature stays fixed. Warm pixels have a higher threshold and can stop a streak.

Playback uses tickInterval = 1 / speed seconds. Changing transfer or baseThreshold affects the current run; changing starting-field parameters resets it.

Starting fields use independent smooth noise values between 0 and 1, generated from seed. With altitude ranging from 0 at the bottom to 1 at the top:

temperature = clamp(1 − altitude + (thermalNoise − 0.5) × temperatureNoise, 0, 1)

water = moisture × waterNoise² × (altitude > 0.62 ? 3 : 0.12)

patchSize sets water-noise spacing in pixels; thermal-noise spacing is 2 × patchSize. The same seed and starting parameters reproduce the same fields.

No replenishment, wind, evaporation, or separate droplets yet. Water eventually settles or drains.
