The rule

Each tick, a cell above its temperature-dependent threshold sends the selected fraction of its water straight down. Every cell reads the old grid, so water moves at most one pixel per tick. Warm cells have higher thresholds and can stop a streak.

Temperature stays fixed. Water starts in noisy upper patches and leaves through the bottom. No replenishment, wind, evaporation, or separate droplets yet: this is a dimensionless rule experiment, not a physical precipitation model.
