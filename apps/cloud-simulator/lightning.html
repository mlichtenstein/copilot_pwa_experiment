Rules and architecture

Each depth has a 12×6 signed charge field in its cloud band, updated every 0.1 simulated seconds. This is an artistic potential proxy, not charge in coulombs or an electrostatic solve. Slow correlated random drift, relaxation and diffusion produce qNext = clamp(q + 0.1 × chargeRate × (chargeBias + 0.7 × bulkDrift + 0.3 × localDrift − 0.025 × q + 0.12 × (neighborMean − q)), −8, 8). Drift itself follows a bounded random walk. chargeBias shifts its mean buildup toward one polarity; either sign can increase the difference from neutral ground. Zero removes this bias. Each layer independently tests gradient = abs(qSource − qTarget) / distance, including neutral ground and opposite-sign cloud regions. When the largest regional gradient reaches breakdownGradient, it grows a channel toward that endpoint. Cloud pairs use aspect-corrected distance and separations from 0.20 to 0.65 scene units to avoid tiny one-cell sparks.

Growth scores seven directions: score = fieldBias × cos(angle − targetAngle) + roughness × (seededNoise + 0.7 × random). branchChance emits short side branches. Contact marks the ancestor path for the bright flash, lowers charge throughout that layer (most strongly near the path), and reduces its charging drift. Flash brightness fades exponentially over flashDuration. The layer then resumes charge buildup; there is no repeat timer. Only ground connections illuminate terrain, with groundLight = flashPower × max(0, 1 − distance / 0.30)². Fixed trees and rocks block a low point light at the ground contact, cutting dark wedges out of that pool. Shadow contrast fades with flash power; cloud strikes create no ground shadows. This is a stylized projected ground plane. Far-to-near cloud/channel/opaque-terrain passes let nearer hills hide farther light.

The scene module owns JSON-serializable state, seeded randomness, dynamics and a read-only renderer. A reusable host owns the fixed-step clock, sliders generated from parameter metadata, fresh setups/reset, rating bar, sharing and canvas. No Lightning-specific branches live in the host. See the scene contract.

Geometry, field scoring and illumination are artistic approximations. No electrostatic solve, leader polarity, upward streamer, charge conservation or thunder propagation is modeled yet. Render resolution is explicit at 160×100; the display enlarges pixels without changing dynamics.
