What is moving?

The three cloud textures are pre-existing CC0 images by WickedInsignia. Their original JPEG pixels are unchanged. Black is empty; grayscale intensity becomes opacity. The browser samples compact 512×512 working copies; no generated cloud detail is added.

Three texture layers drift at different rates over fixed mountain silhouettes. Wind changes texture coordinates. Broad ribbon/oval gradients move independently, revealing and fading parts of the bitmap. A soft height band and mountain-clearance field multiply that opacity:

alpha = texture × opacity × heightBand × mountainClearanceMask × revealMask

This is an artistic texture-and-mask experiment, not a water/air simulation. The broad fields control where texture appears; they do not generate its internal detail. Fields use normalized screen coordinates. Texture switches keep time and parameters; reset restores time 0, Broken puffs, Ribbon + pockets and slider defaults. Play starts paused. Background tabs do not catch up.
