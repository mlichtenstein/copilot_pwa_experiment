What is moving?

Sculpted bank and Layered wisps come from real CC0 photographs by Free Nature Stock and George Becker. On load, they become grayscale shading plus an alpha mask: source RGB is discarded. A soft blue-sky key removes gaps in Sculpted bank; a soft red-channel brightness key removes luminous sky around the dark Layered wisps. These source-specific mattes are approximate, not semantic cloud recognition; similar-colored sky/cloud may be misclassified. The renderer supplies all color. Original grayscale maps remain available.

Windward and leeward clearance affect opposing uphill/downhill slopes according to wind direction. Clearance is limited to irregular pockets, fades on gentle slopes, and vanishes on flats, at peaks and in calm wind. Set one side to zero to let clouds creep to that slope while clearing pockets on the other. This is an artistic terrain interaction, not an orographic airflow solver.

Two independent 3D gradient Perlin fields evolve through time and slide in opposite directions. Their product controls reveal: A = Perlin(x·noiseScale − phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale, phase·0.17); B = Perlin(x·noiseScale·1.7 + phase·noiseDrift, y·noiseScale·1.7, −phase·0.11); phase = time·formationSpeed. Each field also has a fixed seed and layer offset. For speed, each layer samples a 33×21 product grid and interpolates it across the image. reveal = smoothstep(0.5·(1−coverage) − maskSoftness, 0.5·(1−coverage) + maskSoftness, A·B). Coverage 0/1 explicitly clears/removes the cutout. Legacy ribbon/oval masks remain selectable.

alpha = bitmapOpacity × opacity × heightBand × slopeClearanceMask × reveal. Bitmap opacity uses the extracted cloud alpha; source grayscale supplies shading only. Three layers drift at different wind rates. Full sky removes the height band; other placements choose separate centers. Photographs mirror at sampling borders instead of hard wrapping. This is an artistic collage, not a fluid simulation. Reset restores Sculpted bank, Low rolling hills, Full sky, Perlin reveal and time 0. Background tabs do not catch up.
